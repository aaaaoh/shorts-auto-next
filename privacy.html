Privacy Policy

Shorts Auto Next for YouTube

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Stored Data

The extension stores only the following settings locally using Chrome Storage:

  • Auto Next ON/OFF
  • Delay Time
  • Countdown Display ON/OFF

These settings remain on the user's device and are never transmitted to external servers.

Permissions

The extension accesses YouTube pages only to detect the end of Shorts videos and automatically move to the next video.

No browsing history or personal information is collected.
